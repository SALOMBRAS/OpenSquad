# Quality Criteria

## Content
- Narrative coherence across all slides.
- Single explicit CTA in final slide and caption closing.
- Caption opens with a hook and closes clearly.

## Visual
- Readability and contrast are consistently acceptable.
- Typographic hierarchy is consistent.
- Final resolution and framing are correct.

## Technical
- Number of JPG files equals number of slides.
- Zero-padded naming is respected.
- Final publish checkpoint requires `APPROVE`.
